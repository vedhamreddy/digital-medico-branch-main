# A three-minute pitch

**Problem:** emergency treatment often starts without patient history. Scattered records, missed medication and delayed communication leave gaps in care.

**Show the patient portal:** a calm dashboard brings history, allergies, prescriptions and questions together. Mark a dose taken. Ask Doctoc a clinical question; it routes to the doctor instead of guessing.

**Show the doctor portal:** patient history includes the important allergy warning, previous visits and treatment notes. Add a visit note and a medication time. Read and reply to the question. Emergency access logs the clinician and reason; identity verification is explicitly simulated.

**Show patient control:** return to the patient portal to see the new record and reply. Optionally create a second fictional patient account to demonstrate that it starts with a separate, empty record. Turn off doctor access. Open the doctor portal again: the server denies access. Show the patient's audit trail. Re-enable access to restore care.

**Close:** a connected history, adherence tracking and safe communication can make care faster and more informed. This demo proves the workflow with fictional data; production identity, encryption, clinical AI validation and reliable notifications are the next integrations.

The presentation suggests MySQL and biometric authentication. The prototype uses local SQLite and an explicit simulated verification to stay achievable in 24 hours. Doctoc is scripted, not a trained model. Do not claim those production integrations during the pitch.
